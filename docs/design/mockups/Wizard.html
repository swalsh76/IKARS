Set up IKARS

IKARS opens this once, when it finds no settings. Each step checks what it can and says what to do; nothing is sent anywhere, and IKARS never shows a key.

3 · Backups to the NAS share

The ledger and the tax packages are copied each night to a share, in its own ikars\ folder with an IKARS marker. You can skip this and set it later in Settings.

The other steps, in short

1 · Kalshi keyThe two variables IKARS_KALSHI_KEY_ID and IKARS_KALSHI_PRIVATE_KEY_PATH: set or not, the key file found and readable by you only. If missing: how to set them, then "Check again". Never the values.
2 · Engine and feedThe engine runs, its feed is connected, the clock is in step with Kalshi, the ledger verified. If not: what failed, and "Start the engine".
4 · Tax zone and quiet hoursThe tax time zone (the workstation's, America/Denver, unless you choose another) and the quiet hours (22:00 to 07:00).
5 · DoneA summary of what is set and what was skipped; "Start at sign-in" (the background engine and its watchdog) if not installed; then the Overview.
LaterEvery choice can be changed in Settings. The wizard does not come back once the settings file exists.
